# Code of conduct

Tirekick is a small project, and its users are mostly people trying not to get scammed. Treat everyone here the way
you'd want to be treated by someone helping you with that.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, slurs, or sexual or violent content. No personal attacks, and no posting anyone's private
  information, including sellers' names, addresses or photos from listings.
- Keep discussions on topic, and accept a maintainer's decision on what goes into the project.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who
keep breaking them. To report something privately, email support@tirekick.app.
