Spot the scams

  • The same listing reposted with a new code on the title
  • A photo you’ve already seen on a different listing
  • “Text me” and other ways to move you off the site
  • A price far too low against what it sells for
  • Sellers who joined Facebook this year, or have few ratings on eBay
